# Newton School brand guidelines — supplied 2026 reference

Source of truth: [official image](official-2026-guidelines.png), supplied by the Growth team. If this transcription and the image disagree, follow the image. Applies to every Newton School image and video, including frames, thumbnails, captions, logos, and exported variants, regardless of generation tool.

## Logo

- Use the full approved icon + “Newton School” wordmark as one lockup. Never use the icon or wordmark alone, split, redraw, stretch, recolor, or add shadow.
- Clear space: at least half the logo height on all sides. Minimum size: 30 mm in print or 80 px digital.
- On white/light backgrounds use the RGB dark-text signature; on dark/blue backgrounds use the RGB white-text signature. The icon retains its approved blue/turquoise coloring.
- Do not put a pattern, texture, or high-contrast photographic detail beneath or behind the signature.
- The approved standalone assets are stored in `../../creative-engine/memory/brand/assets/`: `newton-school-dark-logo.png` for white/light backgrounds and `newton-school-white-logo.png` for blue/dark backgrounds. Use the supplied lockup intact; do not pass an AI-generated approximation off as the official logo.

## Color

- Primary Brand Blue 500: `#0673F9`.
- Approved secondary 500 values: Orange `#DE5A02`; Purple `#7B55EE`; Green `#009965`; Gold `#F59700`.
- Use one secondary accent per creative. Use the approved palette ramps only for shades and tints. The supplied image shows the full ramps but only these 500 hex values are printed; do not claim exact codes for the other swatches from a visual estimate.
- Approved gradients, top → bottom as shown: Blue `#0673F9` → `#003270`; Green `#50CE99` → `#005C3D`; Purple `#967CFD` → `#221056`; Sand `#FFF3D6` → `#944500`; Blue/navy `#0673F9` → `#0B0C0E`; Orange `#FD9254` → `#A83E00`.
- Neutrals follow the 1000–100 ramp in the supplied reference. Use a legible neutral for text; avoid guessing unprinted hex values.

## Background

- White is preferred. If using another background color, a 10–30% tint is preferred. Full Brand Blue is an approved background with the white-text logo.
- For background darkness of about 40% black or more, use the white-text signature. Avoid backgrounds darker than about 70% black.
- On blue or equivalent backgrounds, use the white-text lockup with sufficient contrast. Subdue photographic or textured backgrounds; keep the logo area visually quiet.

## Typography

- House typeface: **Mona Sans**, used across all communication. The older notes incorrectly said “Mono Sans.”
- Reference hierarchy: heading Semibold 54 px, subhead Light 32 px, body Regular 16 px. Scale proportionally for the format while retaining one-family weight hierarchy.
- Approved Mona Sans variable fonts are stored in `../../creative-engine/memory/brand/assets/`. Use `MonaSans-VariableFont_wdth,wght.ttf` for standard text and `MonaSans-Italic-VariableFont_wdth,wght.ttf` only for intentional italics. When these files are unavailable, use a clearly identified temporary substitute for drafts; final branded exports require the approved typeface.

## Production gate

1. Load this file and the supplied reference image before creating or editing Newton School media in Higgsfield, imagegen, HyperFrames, Remotion, CSS/canvas, or any other tool.
2. Include these constraints in the generation brief. Use deterministic compositing for the real logo and exact factual text; do not rely on generated pixels for exact signatures or claims.
3. Review the final render, including video opening/closing frames and every distinct layout, against logo, color, typography, contrast, and safe-zone rules. Correct deviations before calling it brand-compliant.
4. If the official logo/font or approved claim source is missing, explicitly state the limitation and label the output as a draft. Do not claim strict compliance.
